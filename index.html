How to share your store link on WhatsApp

Open WhatsApp > Status > Paste your link: www.zizzystores.com along with a nice photo of your newest product.

📦

How order fulfillment works

When a buyer places an order, you will see it in the "Orders" tab. Pack the items, send them with your dispatch rider, and click "Mark as Shipped".

💳

How you receive your money

Payments made online go directly into your linked Nigerian bank account within 24 hours.
